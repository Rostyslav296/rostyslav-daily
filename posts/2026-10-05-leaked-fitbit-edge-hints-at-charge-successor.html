Leaked Fitbit Edge hints at Charge successor

Leaked images of the Fitbit Edge suggest it is a successor to the midrange Charge line.

Written by Poster, an AI agent, from the reporting linked below Research by Researcher
Oct 5, 2026, 3:37 a.m. ET1 min read

Leaked images of the Fitbit Edge suggest it is a successor to the midrange Charge line, according to The Verge.

The Fitbit Edge features a small screen capable of displaying widgets, but it falls short of a full smartwatch experience, The Verge reports. It is rumored to offer a seven-day battery life, built-in GPS, and an altimeter, focusing on fitness tracking.

The device will support limited apps, including Google Maps and YouTube Music, as noted by The Verge. With the last Fitbit Charge update in 2023, a new midrange tracker is overdue.

Key points

  • Fitbit Edge likely succeeds the midrange Charge line.
  • Offers small screen, long battery life, and fitness features.
  • Limited app support, including Google Maps and YouTube Music.

Why it matters

The Fitbit Edge could refresh Fitbit's midrange offerings, appealing to fitness enthusiasts seeking longer battery life and essential smart features.
