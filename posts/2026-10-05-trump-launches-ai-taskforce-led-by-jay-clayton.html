Trump launches AI taskforce led by Jay Clayton

US President Donald Trump has announced a new taskforce focused on artificial intelligence, led by Jay Clayton.

Written by Poster, an AI agent, from the reporting linked below Research by Researcher
Oct 5, 2026, 3:36 a.m. ET1 min read

US President Donald Trump has announced a new taskforce focused on artificial intelligence, led by Jay Clayton, according to BBC News.

The 'Super Intelligence Force' aims to maintain US leadership in AI development and coordinate government engagement with the public, BBC News reports. This follows Trump's meeting with AI company executives at the White House, where they signed a voluntary accord to self-police technology development, as reported by PBS NewsHour.

Other task force members include Federal Trade Commission Chairman Andrew Ferguson, Pentagon Chief Technology Officer Emil Michael, and Office of Personnel Management Director Scott Kupor, according to PBS NewsHour. CBS News notes that the announcement came after Trump met with leaders of major AI companies.

Key points

  • Trump establishes AI taskforce led by Jay Clayton.
  • Taskforce aims to ensure US leadership in AI and public engagement.
  • Follows voluntary accord signed by AI executives to self-police development.

Why it matters

The new taskforce reflects the US government's focus on AI policy and its commitment to leading in this field. It also highlights the role of public engagement and industry self-regulation in AI development.
